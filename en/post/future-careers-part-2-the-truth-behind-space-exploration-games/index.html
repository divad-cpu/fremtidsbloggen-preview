The most common career:

What will be the most common career in the future? Different options within the space industry will very likely to be a common practice among earth future graduating students.


The word job will be a word for the past, the word career will be the one most commonly used.


When everything limited associated with its environment the future careers will meet its end of development within careers. Truth to be told there are going to be the same career for centuries when technology has met full demand in every earlier job it can take over to do itself.
