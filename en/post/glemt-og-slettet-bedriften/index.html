Forgotten and Erased: The Business

A device that removes certain memories from your mind.

For example, it can help you enjoy a favourite film you have seen 50 times. It erases all the memories you associate with that film, so that you see your favourite film or series for the first time.

When you are able to erase every memory of every film or series, you can look forward to each one even more and watch them again and again. Will this create a problem for the brain? Will the brain be worn out by repeatedly erasing memories of your favourite films and television series?

The 'Forgotten and Erased' business can also help you with:

- Bad memories

- Attraction to unhealthy food: it changes your thinking and gets you to eat more healthily.

- Mental-health and psychological problems in the mind: the device knows how a healthy brain works, erases the unhealthy brain cells and copies in new healthy cells to replace the harmful ones.

There is not much more a 'forget it' device will help you with — or is there?

But one main concern is mental health. Addressing mental-health problems, which are a major issue in this world, could be accomplished using this device.

Memories create who you are, and if you strike them out, you will deconstruct the many parts on which you are built if you are mostly healthy. Perhaps deconstructing negativity within the mind could help some people.
