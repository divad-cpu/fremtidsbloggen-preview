You Become Through Distance

Who you are at a distance. Who you become at a distance. A distance great enough to make you reconsider.

Who you are at a distance.

Who you become at a distance.

A distance great enough to make you reconsider.

The relationship between you and those around you, in your community, in your nation.

Distances are visible in your opinions. The opinions you may have about your neighbours, the country next door or the people on the other side of the border — do you care?

You have never been so far from your home, and have stayed within the relationships close to you.

Your home, your place: a distance great enough to measure who you are against the differences between you and the people on the other side.

The opinions you hold? Your views of the environment around you, of the world.

What about those on the other side? What is their view of the world, like yours?
