"One Time Enjoyment" or in short: "OTE". IS EVERYTHING ART! "EA"!

Everything is like art= "Everything Art"(EA). Everything we enjoy as "one time enjoyment"(OTE), we can consider as enjoying a moment as like an art painting. Hear me out, when you enjoy your good glass of wine, you can just as good as that wine, listen to a nice song you like, both the song and the wine can be considered as "one time enjoyment"(OTE). You enjoy it one time for a couple of minutes, as you would do with an art objective in an art gallery.

I call it the "one time enjoyment"(OTE). That's everything you do for a couple of minutes as long as its something you consider as one of your favorite things(or it COULD be one of your favorite thing; you just have to try it out first).

As it is with all kinds of art you have "try it out" before you can enjoy it, the same goes for everything that has the possibility to become your favorite thing, either is a new song or a new wine.

I like to look at every new song as new paintings, a new art objective that has been produced. Whether I like it or not is up to my sense of taste.

You could create it by yourself. Do it like a street artist, do it like a Banksy or a Shepard Fairey, smack it on a wall, smack your ideas up, be your creative self-awareness on big cardboards, write, draw, or design then glue it on the wall. Do it small, medium or big, is up to you, choose yourself....... #OTE!

Find everything arts(#EA), do it as "One Time Enjoyment"(OTE) with everything you should be able to with anything. DO IT #OTE!
