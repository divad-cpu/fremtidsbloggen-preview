An Observatory Mind

To observe the world outside and within.

It is about having an observant mindset.

To be observatory.

To have an observatorial mindset.

  • Observatism

  • Observatori

  • Observatore

Observational adaptability

What is true happiness for yourself: to have an observing mindset, to be observatorial?

Observe everyday life. Observe at every hour of the day and night, both consciously and unconsciously. Use your sight and hearing.

Be aware of your own mind and observe what is happening in your thinking, while recognising how it is all connected to what is happening in the environment around you.

Use observatism to become one of those who are self-aware. Practising observatism for your own benefit can lead you on an inner journey full of self-insight, and toward a perspective grounded in understanding the world outside and yourself within.

Become aware of what is said to be infinite, but in fact has an inner and an outer limit: a universal way of thinking logically about how everything around us — the physical world and what we say and reflect upon — has a limit in relation to time.

The fact that you, as a human being, have been given the opportunity to observe what is around you and what is happening inside you is one way to become 100% Sentinel.

Practising observatism in combination with logic, rationality, reason and understanding can make you someone who is open to encountering all kinds of knowledge and opinions.

You will be able to learn and practise genuine, natural curiosity. This means being someone who can master new things and learn in life's lifelong school.

Observe in, out, in, out and all around with sight and hearing.
