The Magic of Technology

Where can technology make magic real? What tricks will we see brought to life?

Where do we need magic to become real through technology?

What tricks will we see become reality through technology?

The magic of: Creating new technology Creating environmentally friendly technology

Who can create?

Who can create? You can create, your friends can make things, and your family and colleagues can create. Every person with an interest or passion can create. An interest can be the beginning of new technology. Having a passion for what you do is enough.

What can you create?

I use the word “anything”. It means endless possibilities for you. Endless possibilities mean that if you have an idea, or even just an interest, you can create something significant. Make something beautiful, create something meaningful, create an opportunity, create a new beginning. Be among the creators: the creators of our technologies, the creators of our time.

To help, through technology

To help people

Who can help?

All of you can help. Technology can help. You can help with the aid of technology. Technology will eventually be a key in this area.

Whom should we help, and where?

People, animals and the climate. In our homes, at work and outdoors. Out in the jungle, in the forest, in nature.

How do we help?

Through technology, of course. Through human engagement. Through cooperation and communication between communities.

To learn from technology

To teach children

Who can learn?

Every man and woman can learn. Every boy and girl can learn. Learning can take place at any age, wherever you come from.

Who should learn?

From children to adults. Knowledge is here to stay: to live in our communities, live with our families and teach the next generation.

How do we listen and learn?

GO TO SCHOOL. Or read. Read about the things that matter to you, or that matter to the problems that need solving.

Get an education. Learn at school, from the internet, from the people around you and by following your interests. Find your passion.

Become educated enough to get ideas for improving schools, teaching methods and the way you teach.

To build with technology

To build a better society

Who can build?

Governments, organisations and industries. Those with resources. Those with ideas. Those with commitment. Through cooperation.

What can they build?

A better future. A better nation. A better city. And a better society. A city of opportunity, employment and stability.

Build safety, build education systems, build modern infrastructure suited to a modern city. With the help of technology, we should be aware and prepared.

Above I have written about what I call tech magic: the moment when technology is invented and the first glimpse of it feels like magic.
