A Design Is Meant to Be

A design shaped by a logical ideology. The most sensible design. A design that serves people.

A design shaped by a logical ideology.

The most sensible design.

A design that serves people.

A design is meant to give us joy. It is meant to make us comfortable.

A design is meant to be something that is assumed to be a certain way. It is meant to look that way because it was the only way a design could serve the purpose for which it was created.

A design is meant to have the ability to be meaningful, to be flawless.

Creations, creative solutions, credibility, the creation of beauty, but also usefulness.

Creating something from an idea.

Imagine being quick, willing to be associated with the next brand, the next design — simply being that.

Be the first by designing a design meant to be.
