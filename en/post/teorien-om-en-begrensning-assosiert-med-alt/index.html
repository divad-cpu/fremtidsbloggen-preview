Based on biological formation:

There is a determined mathematical explanation for reproduction and its biological result. The mathematical and chemical formula determines a receiver and sender in reproduction. Those two were themselves created through the same process, so current biological diversity is produced by the earliest receiver and sender: a mathematical and chemical sequence beginning with the first reproduction of life.

A human cannot reproduce with an elephant; biological diversity is determined to reproduce within its own species.

There are different combinations within species. Reproduction between receiver and sender of the same species, described mathematically, produces a finite number of possible visual physical designs.

Collect data on friendly, positive, and unfriendly, negative, visually and physically possible designs across mathematical forms, together with their alphabetical descriptions, to obtain the finite number.
