The Responsibility

It is our responsibility to understand. To understand individuals. To understand responsibility.

It is our responsibility to understand.

To understand individuals.

To understand responsibility.

An industry responsible for expanding and ensuring that there is enough employment within the system, within the infrastructure of a country's economy. To be responsible for your industry, you must use your knowledge. Be able to use your abilities to form an understandable perception and exercise fair judgement.

You must see how the industry affects its surroundings. The environment is everything; all the things that need our attention and human affection are your area of responsibility.

Your attention is needed when something must be improved or is wrong. Improved in a way that only you can handle, because you are the person who understands the matter. If something is wrong, you must be the better person and take responsibility.

How you work in the industry is a matter for you and your colleagues. It is a matter of who you are in the industry. The industry you work for is responsible for the people in the economy, for customers and for the whole picture as an institution of development in the area where it operates. Industry is the key to moving forward in this century with the help of its employees, governments, customers and the local community.

Industries can make things happen. They can improve our livelihoods. They improve through technology and through the people who work for them. There is a common interest between an industry, its employees and its customers: the interest of those who are responsible.

One is responsible for educating. One is responsible for creating. One is responsible for developing.

To educate people, to educate older people, to educate the younger generation, to educate and improve our civilisation.

To create opportunities, to create a stable economy, to create better alternatives for this generation and the next.

To develop a common interest, an interest shared in a modern society — a modern society shaped by people as those responsible. The people with this responsibility are the key structure of our modern society.

It is a shared responsibility to behave fairly, act with justice and behave responsibly.
