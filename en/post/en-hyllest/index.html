A Tribute

To a television series. To Rick and Morty, to be precise. A series with style. One system, hundreds of variations.

To a television series.

To Rick and Morty, to be precise.

A series with style. One system, hundreds of variations.

A fantastic and incredible series. For those who know it, it is unique. For those who do not, it should be taken in visually, by the part of the brain that absorbs the visual molecules of Rick and Morty.

It is not only based on a scientist, Rick, and his grandson and faithful partner at times, Morty, who venture into the great space beyond Earth and into other dimensions and multiverses. They experience the most imaginative things a human brain is capable of developing.

Thanks to creators Justin Roiland and Dan Harmon, these two imaginative people have created a television series that is a mixture full of creatures, expressions and surroundings that make you wonder, as well as a story that should capture anyone interested in the subject.

The series follows a certain line concerning the lives of Rick and Morty. It is a fantastic conception of what imagination can accomplish in a human brain.

Absolutely incredible. What a series — a tribute was called for.

The visual molecules are absorbed into my genetic brain every time I take a look.

Can Rick Sanchez, the scientist, become or be a role model for people today? As a scientist, researcher, mathematician or theorist? A computer engineer? As a grandfather or father? As an adventurer, traveller or explorer of what exists beyond this planet, or on it? Or as a personality who allows someone to feel like themselves and who they are, rather than what you or others think you are? You should know who you are and do the things that seem reasonable to you and those around you.

Check it out on Netflix.
