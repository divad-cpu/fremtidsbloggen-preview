When Everything Is Calculated

When everything can be calculated and predicted by computing power.

When the data that is being collected becomes predictable.

Predictions, predictions: how do we benefit from them? One possible outcome could be a threatening consequence for humanity. What could possibly be so threatening? Earthquakes, tsunamis, volcanoes — every natural disaster can be calculated and predicted using data. Pandemics or economic consequences are another dilemma. It is a question of having enough data and the right data. When enough of the right data is brought together in a supercomputer, it may make it possible to generate very close dates for when a disaster will occur. We have some tools for predicting natural disasters today, but they are not good or accurate enough. Forecasts must be accurate within a year for us to be sufficiently prepared. This is possible with accurate, measurable and enough correct data.

The method of predictive power using data and mathematical calculations.

Predicting through mathematics combined with data makes this a predictable structure.

Testing prototypes becomes something that comes with age.

Using data and a mathematical engine, you only need to enter your prototype and see every possible outcome of what would have happened to it in reality, in every possible scenario and situation. This is already happening in some way today, but development in this field will be combined with innovation, becoming an important tool for seeing the results from every possible angle of an idea or prototype.

Try to imagine what we could use prediction for, what kind of data and how much data: the configuration of the calculation process.
