A Good Podcast #1 and Song of the Day: 23 September 2020

Time Is Money — a podcast with Peter Warren, who has many years of experience in trading, asset management, investments and finance.

Time Is Money — a podcast with Peter Warren.

Peter Warren has many years of experience in trading, asset management, investments and most areas of finance.

They offer many valuable perspectives on what is happening in the Norwegian and global markets. They discuss current situations, what may happen and what has happened in the past. The subjects are mostly finance and business, presented in an accessible way for those who are interested.

Recommended.
