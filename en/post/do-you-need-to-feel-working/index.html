Do You Need to Feel Like Working?

Do you have to feel like starting work? Do you need to want to work on projects? There is a difference between wanting to and doing.

Do you have to feel like it to start working?

Do you need to want to or feel like working on projects?

There is a difference between wanting to and doing.

I want to work. But my body says no. I say yes, but my brain says no. I think yes, but the rest of me says no.

What is wrong with me, then, when I — me, David — say yes to starting work on projects?

- Is it the environment?

- Is it the attitude within the environment?

I think that in the right environment, I will change my attitude and push myself in a good way to start working.

Can places you often spend time in become used up? Places like home and work.

Yes, from time to time.

Timing is everything: knowing when and where to work, either out or at home.
