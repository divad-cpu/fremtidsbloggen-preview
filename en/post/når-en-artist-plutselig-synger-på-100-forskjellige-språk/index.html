When an AI is capable of changing the same song into 100 different languages.

The artist originally sings in English or Norwegian. But with an advanced AI, it will sound as if the artist can sing in 100 different languages. The song sounds completely authentic. It sounds as if the artist is singing it, while in reality the AI has changed the language and adapted it so that everything matches the melody and lyrics.

What will this lead to? An enormous change throughout the music industry. Suddenly there will be a host of global stars, provided, of course, that the song itself is good enough for the audience. But the probability of a song reaching the charts in one country or another is probably quite high. Perhaps Karpe Diem will become one of the biggest artists in a country such as Indonesia or Uruguay.

It will open the way for more people to make a living from their music. Music from every corner of the world will spread. The value of the music industry will most likely increase alongside this technological development.

Imagine a Spotify where you can choose the language in which the artist sings.

You have a great song in Norwegian that you would like to show your friends in Korea. No problem: just switch to Korean.

Will future technology also enable an artist to sing in a local language in Kenya during a concert? Perhaps.
