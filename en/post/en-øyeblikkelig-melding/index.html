An Instant Message

A push, a command through a digital assistant, and within milliseconds the message is delivered to everyone with an online electronic device...

A push, a command through a digital assistant, and within milliseconds the message is delivered to everyone with an online electronic device — a message delivered to each and every person. Whatever kind of app you have, this message needs only an internet connection. A song, an important text message, a video: it does not matter what the message contains.

An instant message delivered to each and every person with only an internet connection.

It appears like nothing else, only to remain on a device as any bit, megabit, gigabit, terabit or petabit.

At the push of a button, the machine takes control of your device to fascinate the person who is out of reach, perhaps in another country, an authoritarian state.

A hacker, a group, a powerful state.

There are things at stake when an instant message multiplies and makes its way.
