The “I Invented That Kind of Drone” Drone

A drone that does your chores, watches over your children, or helps with other everyday tasks.

Oh, that kind of drone: a drone that does chores at home. A drone that flies over your children to watch over them, reading and predicting danger in their surroundings. An AI security drone.

The conception of drones

An autonomous system, of course.

An analysis system for the drones

A fishing drone

A drone that fishes in the sea

An art drone on autopilot

A drone with built-in paint that paints walls

A mini automated drone with a screen that flies beside you

You can easily read and interact with the screen

A device that displays text when you give a speech

A small drone with a screen that follows a person speaking to an audience

A flying sign with adjustable words that lets people know what you think. If you do something silly in a car, the sign says “sorry”.

A small drone with a visible display that tells other drivers what you think, or that you are sorry or grateful.

Drones that vacuum using a digital map and sensors

Or cleaning drones

They collect dust through a tube or a magnetic dust collector

A security drone for home and business. Place sensors in the areas where you want your security drone to monitor for intruders.

A security drone that flies from its base near the house when sensors around the house detect something

A drone that waters your plants

Small drones with small water containers that water your plants, flying from a convenient base in your home

The same drones could work in your garden with a base outside.

A drone that gardens, trimming and maintaining your plants

A drone with a wireless charging base: surely there are possibilities here?

A drone base with wireless charging and interchangeable drones: one charges while another works

Several mini work drones with different tasks, such as putting tools away

Drones for a workshop, such as a car workshop, flying back and forth to fetch tools

A fan drone

A small autonomous drone that cools you in the summer heat

A disinfection drone that detects where germs are and sprays the specific area

An umbrella or rain-cover drone

An assembly drone with sensors?

A garden drone with guidance software

A traffic drone

A mapping drone that updates street maps, flying around cities and places to map and photograph them, like Google Maps

A drone basket

Home drones

A small drone with a camera and speakers

Hotel helper drones

A mini drone with a screen and voice assistant to help with everything at the hotel, plus a safety mechanism preventing it from being taken out of the room or hotel

A fire drone that detects and extinguishes small fires before they become large

A mini vacuum drone that autonomously detects dust and particles

Drone speakers

A vacuum drone that picks up gravel from a driveway, road or pavement after winter

A drone with wheels

Rain-collecting drones with large screens to collect water

A drone that checks roofs for leaks, with a device below the roof and a drone above that photographs or scans it

A self-tanning drone that sprays and applies tanning product or sunscreen to your body

Drones that fill drinks

Bar drones that refill drinks or fill a shot glass. A mark or sensor in the bottom centre of the glass tells the drone where to pour.

Drones for everything, drones for everyone.
