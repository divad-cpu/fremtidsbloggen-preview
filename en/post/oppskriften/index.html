The Recipe

There is a recipe for everything: the universe, the planets, the Earth, life, people and plants.

There is a recipe for everything. A recipe for the universe, the planets, the Earth, life, people and plants.

If the recipe were a God, it would have created everything according to its own recipe as the first recipe for everything.

There is a recipe for how life unfolds, how we develop from children into adults and how we behave as humans.

The recipe for our own personal development is decisive for everyone around us and, not least, ourselves.

There is a recipe for becoming rich and for becoming poor.

There is a recipe for having tonnes of food produced and developed; there is a reverse 'negative recipe' that results in a lack of food and water for the population.

There is a 'negative recipe' for creating war between nations, between people and civil wars.

A 'negative recipe' for a crisis such as an epidemic or natural disaster.

Nature has its own recipe for survival, for creating more trees, plants and wildlife.

There are recipes that have not yet been discovered, inside someone's head or brain, future brains, or in the empty 'space' that nobody has ever thought about: an unknown 'space' where all future ideas lie ahead of our time.

A recipe for exploring planets and developing spaceships.

A recipe for humanity in deep space, with new planets as future colonies.

What if there were a 'cookbook' for every kind of personal development on Earth, from poverty to wealth, from depression to happiness?

A 'cookbook' with recipes for how people can develop creative thoughts to use for work, research, school, studies or some form of innovation.

There is a universal recipe for everything.
