About Fremtidsbloggen

A place to think differently.

This website is about giving you a new experience of reading a blog that you may find futuristic.

I want to bring something futuristic and full of ideas into your imagination.

I want to give you something to chew on and think about.

What comes next in this real world?

What will stay the same, and what will change for the better for us on this planet?

Is it all about technology, is it about freedom, or is it about humanity's progress on a greater, modern journey through time?

Fremtidsbloggen is a place to pause, reflect and think ahead.

We think about technology, read it, hear it, talk about it and write it.

It is about speculating from different perspectives on what is happening in our society today and tomorrow.

For those with a particular interest.
